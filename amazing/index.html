Beyond the Finish Line: Our Vision for a Purpose-Driven Community
超越終點線：建立有使命感的社群

In the heart of Hong Kong’s competitive dragon boat circuit, our team is proving that the strength of a stroke isn't just about physical power, it’s about the heart behind it. Team Amazing 「阿美勝」, a vibrant crew of part-time athletes, has established a mission that extends far beyond the trophies and podiums of our recent races.

A Rhythm Rooted in Faith
根植於信仰的節奏
▼
At the core of Team Amazing is a profound commitment to Christian values. We define our "Mission & Goal" as using the sport of dragon boating to manifest Christ’s love within the community. The boat is more than a vessel; we build it to be a sanctuary where unity and cooperation are practiced as acts of worship. At the same time, we recognize that not all of our teammates share the same faith. Yet when we paddle together, we move as one crew, united in purpose and striving toward a common goal. In this shared rhythm, we believe that one day, through the harmony and fellowship experienced on the boat, every teammate may come to encounter the depth of God’s love revealed in our unity.
From Teamwork to Community Contribution
從團隊合作到貢獻龍舟社群
▼
Our vision for contributing to the dragon boat community is built on the principle of mutual trust. We believe that by fostering a culture where members care for one another, we create a ripple effect that touches the wider society. This isn't just theory; it’s a call to action. To extend this fellowship, we welcome and invite other teams to practice alongside us on the water. Through these shared training sessions, our goal is to let others experience the distinct warmth, unity, and support that define a crew rooted in Christian values. We seek to be a vibrant team that takes the sport seriously while remaining deeply anchored in fellowship and perseverance.
Spreading the Light
將光明傳播到社區
▼
Looking ahead, our wish is to bridge the gap between competitive sports and social service. Even as we juggle our professional responsibilities with our passion for paddling, we remain focused on showing that every race is more than competition; it is an opportunity to serve. By keeping our eyes on the "Goal" and our hearts on the "Mission," we are transforming the dragon boat from a traditional cultural icon into a modern vehicle for faith, hope, and love. For Team Amazing, the ultimate victory isn't found at the finish line, but in the lives touched along the way.
Connecting All Nations
走向萬民
▼
Beyond local waters, we actively connect with the global dragon boat community both online and offline. On the water, we seek to participate in international races and build cross-border fellowships with teams worldwide. Digitally, we have built a thriving global Facebook group of over 20,000 subscribers - Dragon Boat Lifestyle . This platform serves as a hub for paddlers worldwide to share training techniques, while allowing us a unique opportunity to occasionally share uplifting Christian messages and values with the global community.

Frequently Asked Questions

常見問題 Q&A

Q1: Is Team Amazing only for Christians? Do I have to be a Christian to join? Q1: 阿美勝龍舟隊只招收基督徒嗎？我必須是基督徒才能加入嗎？ ▼

Absolutely not. While our leadership team is anchored in Christian values and we operate as a faith-based fellowship, Team Amazing is proudly built on radical inclusivity. We welcome paddlers from all backgrounds, beliefs, cultures, and walks of life. The only requirement is a shared commitment to sportsmanship, mutual respect, and pulling together in the boat.

絕對不是。雖然我們的管理團隊以基督教價值觀為核心，且整個團隊建立在信仰團契的基礎上，但阿美勝一直以來都秉持極大包容的理念。我們非常歡迎來自不同背景、信仰、文化和各行各業的划手加入。我們唯一的條件，是大家對體育精神的共同追求、彼此尊重，並在同一條船上齊心協力、同舟共濟。

Q2: What does the name "阿美勝" mean? Q2: 「阿美勝」這個名字有什麼特殊含意嗎？ ▼

"阿美勝" is the phonetic transliteration of "Amazing." For us, it represents the amazing grace, strength, and fellowship we experience as a team. It reminds us that when a crew operates with shared values and a unified heart, the community we build together is truly amazing.

「阿美勝」是英文「Amazing」的廣東話譯音。對我們而言，它代表著我們在團隊中共同體驗到的奇妙恩典（Amazing Grace）、力量與團契情誼。它時刻提醒著我們，當所有隊員帶著共同的價值觀和合一的心並肩作戰時，我們共同建立的團隊與凝聚力，就是最不可思議、最「Amazing」的見證。

Q3: How do you balance the religious side with high-intensity sports training? Q3: 你們如何平衡信仰元素與高強度的體育競技訓練？ ▼

We don't see them as separate, they reinforce each other! The discipline, perseverance, and resilience required to be an elite dragon boat paddler are the exact same qualities we cultivate in our personal and spiritual growth. We train hard on the water to win races, and we use our gathering times off the water to support each other's lives through fellowship and small groups.

我們從不把這兩者分開看待。相反，它們是相輔相成的！要成為一名龍舟划手，所需要的自律、堅持與韌性，正正與我們在個人生命及信仰成長中所鍛煉的精神完美契合。我們在水上拼盡全力訓練以贏得比賽；在岸上，我們透過團契和小組聚會互相關心、彼此扶持，全方位支持每位隊員的生命。

Q4: If you had just 30 seconds to explain what Team Amazing is, what would you say? Q4: 如果你只有30秒時間解釋什麼是「阿美勝」，你會怎麼說？ ▼

Team Amazing is a purpose-driven dragon boat community rooted in Christian values. As part-time athletes, we train hard on the water to pursue athletic excellence while building deep mutual trust. Off the water, our fellowship supports each other's life journeys and spreads love to the community. We welcome paddlers of all faiths and backgrounds to paddle as one crew. For us, the ultimate victory isn’t crossing the finish line first, it’s the lives touched and lifelong friendships built along the way.

「阿美勝」是一個植根於基督教價值觀、充滿使命感的龍舟社群。作為業餘運動員，我們在水上訓練、追求卓越，建立深厚默契；在岸上，我們透過團契生活彼此支持，並將基督的愛帶進社區。無論你有何信仰背景，我們都歡迎你同舟共濟。對我們而言，真正的勝利不在於率先衝線，而是在旅程中彼此扶持、觸動生命，並建立一生的摯友情誼。
