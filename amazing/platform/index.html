Vision 2027 Concept Pitch

The Amazing Platform
阿美勝平台 2027

Expanding from a single sport into an interconnected community ecosystem where every member has something to offer and every member can find support.

阿美勝正從單一運動發展成為一個相互聯繫的網絡，每個成員都能貢獻一己之長，亦能得到合適的支持。

The Ecosystem Concept | 生態圈理念

Paddling together was just Phase 1. Now, we are taking Team Amazing to the next stage.

Phase 1: The First "App"

When people hear Team Amazing, they usually think of dragon boat racing. Think of dragon boating as our very first "app" in an app store.

每當大家聽到「Team Amazing」，通常會聯想到龍舟比賽，但龍舟只是我們的第一個「App」。

Phase 2: The Platform

An ecosystem of human support allowing members to share diverse talents, from non-profit pickleball coaching to helping teammates recovering from surgery.

一個相互聯繫的網絡，讓隊員分享多元技能，並在身心需要時隨時伸出援手。

Platform Core Purpose | 平台宗旨

Unlocking the collective strength of our community

集合我們生態圈中的集體力量

Empower Skills

Enabling members (like certified coaches or experienced mentors) to host free or non-profit classes for the group.

賦能技能： 使成員（如認證教練或導師）能為團體舉辦免費或非營利課程。

Enable Support

Providing a trusted channel for requesting and contributing talents such as assisting teammates during health recovery or daily logistics.

支援同行： 提供值得信賴的管道，例如於成員手術後康復或需要雜務協助時提供志願支援。

Strengthen Bonds

Transforming casual sports teammates into a resilient, deeply caring, and lifelong supportive network.

加強連結： 將一般體育隊友轉化為堅韌且互相關懷的生命網絡。

Serving a Higher Purpose | 終極使命

Ultimately, the Amazing Platform fulfills the Great Commission of Jesus Christ to "go and make disciples of all nations." Through our community, we aim to share the Gospel, guide members into an authentic relationship with Christ, and empower one another to live as His disciples.

阿美勝平台旨在履行耶穌基督的大使命 (“你們要去，使萬民作我的門徒。”)。透過我們的社群，我們希望分享福音，引導成員建立與基督的關係，並互相鼓勵成為祂的門徒。
