## 🚣‍♂️ 我們的願景與宗旨 / Our Vision & Mission

*   **根植於信仰 (Rooted in Faith):** 通過龍舟運動促進隊員之間的團結與合作，並在社區中彰顯基督的愛。We use the sport of dragon boating to manifest Christ’s love and unity within the local community.
*   **同心向前 (Moving in One Beat):** Fostering a vibrant community that moves together as one crew (*Amazing One!*), achieving excellence on the water while promoting Christian values and mutual trust off the water (*Amazing All in One!*).
