# Team Amazing – Dragon Boat Team Portfolio

Welcome to the official repository for **Team Amazing**, a Hong Kong-based competitive dragon boat team dedicated to building a purpose-driven community through faith and sportsmanship.

## 🚣‍♂️ Our Vision
To foster a community that moves in "One Beat," achieving excellence on the water while promoting Christian values and service off the water.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Framework:** HTML5 / CSS3 (Tailwind CSS)
- **Deployment:** GitHub Pages
- **Design:** Figma

## 📁 Project Structure
- `/img`: High-resolution team photos and brand assets.
- `index.html`: The main landing page showcasing our mission and results.
- `sitemap.xml`: SEO optimization for search engines.

## 📈 Goals for 2026-2027
- Launching integrated Instagram/Facebook community feeds.
- Reach out to the Dragon Boat community
- Expanding community outreach programs in Hong Kong.
